02 / PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Your computer is not a data source.

Veronica reads local system services and files to help you. Your clipboard, activity, credentials and usage history are not uploaded to Veronica servers—because there are no Veronica servers.

  • ✓ No account or licence key
  • ✓ No analytics or tracking
  • ✓ Open source under GPL-3.0
