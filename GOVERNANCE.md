# Governance

## Maintainers

Veronica is maintained by [Naman Dadhich](https://github.com/namannn04) and
[Pulkit](https://github.com/pulkitxm). Together they decide the project's
technical direction, manage releases and repository permissions, and handle
security reports. Naman owns the repository and has the final say when the
maintainers disagree.

## Decisions

Routine changes are decided through focused issues and pull requests. Larger
features, packaging changes, and architectural work should begin with a public
issue so the goals and tradeoffs can be discussed before implementation.
Decisions favor user privacy, safe control of the user's machine, a fast and
native desktop experience, and long-term maintainability.

When consensus is not clear, a maintainer records the decision and its reasons
in the relevant issue or pull request. Security-sensitive decisions may remain
private until coordinated disclosure is complete.

## Contributions and Maintainers

Anyone may report issues, propose changes, review pull requests, or improve
documentation. Repository permissions may be extended to contributors who show
sustained, constructive participation, sound judgment, and care for the
project's users. Access can be reduced when a maintainer becomes inactive or no
longer follows the Code of Conduct or security practices.

Contributors keep the copyright in their work and provide it under the
project's license, the GNU General Public License version 3 with the additional
terms in [NOTICE](NOTICE), when their contribution is accepted.
